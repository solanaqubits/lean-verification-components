# Quantum algebra, photonics, and mechanics

[All sections](../README.md)

## Modules

| Module | Verified result |
|---|---|
| [SpinPhotonicWaveguide](SpinPhotonicWaveguide.en.md) | Nonnegative four-coordinate energy, preservation under specified mixing, and phase and geometric-profile formulas. |
| [NonHermitianPhotonicEP](NonHermitianPhotonicEP.en.md) | The sign of κ²−γ², its vanishing at κ=γ, and real splitting 2√Δ. No operator or complex spectrum is constructed. |
| [QuantumTransmonEngine](QuantumTransmonEngine.en.md) | A lower bound for a prescribed two-level gap and exact deviations from ng=1/2. |
| [QuantumHeatEngine](QuantumHeatEngine.en.md) | Prescribed scalar heat and work in an Otto cycle, efficiency in (0,1), and a conditional comparison with Carnot efficiency. |
| [QuantumStabilizerCodes](QuantumStabilizerCodes.en.md) | Phase-free Boolean Pauli representation, symmetry of its product, and four distinct syndromes for a three-qubit bit-flip code. |
| [QuantumCliffordTableau](QuantumCliffordTableau.en.md) | H, S, and CNOT preserve a Boolean symplectic form; basis actions and involutions in the phase-free representation. |
| [QuantumToricCode](QuantumToricCode.en.md) | Parity of a prescribed overlap, energy formulas, and addition of a defect pair by definition. No lattice is constructed. |
| [NonAbelianHolonomy](NonAbelianHolonomy.en.md) | Real skew-symmetric 3×3 matrices: closure under commutators, so(3) relations, and Jacobi. Parallel transport along loops is not defined. |
| [SpintronicTransport](SpintronicTransport.en.md) | A prescribed antisymmetric response matrix, zero scalar work, and an inverse matrix for a nonzero coefficient. |
| [SymplecticHamiltonianDynamics](SymplecticHamiltonianDynamics.en.md) | The prescribed symplectic Euler step has determinant one and preserves a modified quadratic energy, nonnegative when h²k < 4m. |
| [QuantumMajoranaChain](QuantumMajoranaChain.en.md) | Explicit complex 2×2 matrices: Majorana algebra, CAR, a number projection, and involutive parity. No spatial chain is constructed. |

## Scope

The results concern the explicit matrices, transformations, and scalar energy models. Their correspondence to a physical device or a full many-body system is not established by compilation.
